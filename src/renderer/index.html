Welcome to Tlonbot

Tlonbot connects an AI assistant to your Tlon identity through a local Urbit moon. It runs quietly in your menubar.

This wizard will guide you through:

  1. Entering your moon credentials from Horizon
  2. Choosing an AI provider and model
  3. Downloading the Urbit runtime and booting your moon

The whole process takes a few minutes.

Moon Credentials

You'll need a moon from your Tlon ship. Follow these steps to generate one:

  1. Go to horizon.tlon.network and log in
  2. Navigate to /apps/webterm
  3. Run |moon in the terminal
  4. Copy the moon name and key string from the output
